Frequently Asked

Everything you need to know before getting started.

Are these real legal contracts valid in Pakistan? +
Yes. All documents are drafted under Pakistani law by a practising Advocate High Court. They are legally valid, enforceable, and court-ready.
How do I pay? +
We accept bank transfer, Easypaisa, JazzCash, and Raast. Payment details are shared after you confirm your requirement. Documents delivered after payment confirmation.
Can I get a contract urgently? +
Yes. Urgent delivery (12 hours) is available at +50% on the standard price. Just mention "urgent" when you contact us and we will prioritise immediately.
Is my information kept confidential? +
Absolutely. All information shared with us is strictly confidential. We are bound by professional legal ethics as Advocates. Your documents and details are never shared with third parties.
Do you offer revisions? +
Yes — one free revision is included with every document. Revisions are delivered within 24 hours. Additional revisions can be arranged at a small fee.
Can you draft contracts for IT companies? +
Yes. We specialise in IT sector contracts — software development agreements, IT service contracts, NDA for tech businesses, freelancer agreements, and employment contracts for IT firms. We understand PSEB and IT export requirements.
