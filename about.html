About DASTAWAIZ

Pakistan's Digital Document Platform — by an Advocate

Our Mission

To make professional legal documents accessible to every Pakistani business and individual — regardless of budget, location or legal knowledge.

The Problem We Solve

Every day, businesses sign poorly drafted contracts, landlords lose rent disputes over missing clauses, and freelancers work with no written protection — because legal help is expensive, slow and hard to reach. DASTAWAIZ combines technology with the experience of a practising lawyer to deliver clear, Pakistan-focused documents fast and affordably.
